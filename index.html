Free museum art you can actually print

Museums like The Met, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Cleveland Museum of Art and the Rijksmuseum have released hundreds of thousands of public-domain images for anyone to use. The hard part is knowing which files are big enough to print well. Every work listed here shows the true pixel size of the museum's file and the largest print you can make from it.
