Licensing and sources

Every image on this site is hotlinked from a museum that released it as public domain. We do not host or alter the files.

The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Images marked public domain in The Met's Open Access programme are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0): you may copy, modify and use them, including commercially, without permission. Data comes from the Met Collection API.

Art Institute of Chicago

Public-domain works in the Art Institute of Chicago's open access collection are published under CC0.

Rijksmuseum

The Rijksmuseum makes public-domain images in its collection freely available for reuse.

Things to check
