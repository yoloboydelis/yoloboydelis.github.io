About

Free Art Prints is an independent guide to public-domain art from museum open-access collections. For each work we measure the museum's full-resolution file and calculate how large you can print it, so you don't pay for a blurry poster.

All data comes directly from museum APIs; nothing is invented. Images stay on the museums' servers.
